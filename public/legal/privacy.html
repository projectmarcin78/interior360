Privacy & cookies

Effective: 21 October 2026

This notice describes the current preview. Public submission forms are disabled. Before activating the Google-backed directory, we will update this notice to describe its data flows, legal bases and retention periods.

Who to contact

Interior360 sp. z o.o. operates the website. For privacy requests, contact marcin@interior360.pl. Do not include identity documents or sensitive information in a public review or an initial enquiry.

Browsing and hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs visitors' IP addresses for security. Hosting and communications providers process technical information under their applicable terms. See GitHub's privacy statement for provider practices, international processing, safeguards and retention. We do not have direct access through this site's code to GitHub's visitor security logs.

Request helper

The current preview shows an AI assistant that is not connected. Its request field and button are disabled. It does not send requests to an AI provider or store them. Manual service filters work in your browser. Before enabling it, we will update this notice with the provider, purpose, legal basis, data flows, safeguards and retention details. Do not enter private or sensitive information in the request field.

Cookies and external services

The preview's own code does not set cookies, use browser storage, install analytics, load advertising trackers or embed YouTube videos. WhatsApp and other external contacts are ordinary links: the external service is opened when you click. Its own privacy rules then apply. See WhatsApp's privacy policy. This statement concerns our code, not a promise that hosting or external providers never process data.

Adding a Google app, video embeds, analytics or advertising may change storage and tracking. We will review this before activation, update the notice and obtain prior consent where required. A consent banner alone does not prevent tracking; optional trackers must actually remain blocked until consent.

Messages and reports

If you email or WhatsApp us, we receive the details you choose to provide, including contact information and message content. We use enquiries to respond to your request or take steps toward a requested service, where Article 6(1)(b) GDPR applies. We handle general reports, security and correspondence on our legitimate interests in administering and protecting the service, subject to your rights, and comply with legal duties where Article 6(1)(c) applies. We do not use these enquiries for unrelated marketing without a separate lawful basis.

Correspondence is reviewed when a matter closes and deleted when no longer necessary. Where a legal duty or a specific dispute requires retention, the relevant records are kept only for that purpose and the applicable period. Provider-controlled retention is described in provider notices.

Your rights

Depending on the processing and legal basis, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, object to legitimate-interest processing, and withdraw any consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. We may need proportionate information to verify a request. You may complain to your supervisory authority, including Poland's UODO or Italy's Garante. These rights are subject to the conditions and exceptions in the GDPR.
