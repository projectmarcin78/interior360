Privacy & cookies

Effective: 21 October 2026

SicilyCrew uses Google Apps Script for its directory and submission forms, with records held in a private Google Sheet. Approved contacts and reviews are shared only with invited members in the protected directory. AI assistance is disabled.

Who to contact

Interior360 sp. z o.o. operates the website. For privacy requests, contact marcin@interior360.pl. Do not include identity documents or sensitive information in a shared review or an initial enquiry.

Browsing and hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs visitors' IP addresses for security. Hosting and communications providers process technical information under their applicable terms. See GitHub's privacy statement for provider practices, international processing, safeguards and retention. We do not have direct access through this site's code to GitHub's visitor security logs.

Contacts, reviews and moderation

Contact submissions contain the name, selected services, base town, areas served, languages, contact phone number and description you provide. Reviews contain a display name, rating, service month and comment linked to a contact. We also record a generated record ID, submission time and moderation status. No email address, identity document or account registration is required by these forms. Access requires an administrator-approved phone number and a one-time code sent personally by the administrator on WhatsApp.

Information comes from the person submitting it, who may be the business owner, a customer or another community member. Share contact details only where you are entitled to share them with this group. Do not assume a private directory removes the need to respect a person’s privacy or objection. Our legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR are maintaining a useful local directory, enabling community feedback, assessing reports and preventing misuse. We assess relevance and privacy before sharing and consider objections individually. Agreeing to the submission declaration does not remove anyone else’s data-protection rights.

Pending and rejected submissions are visible only to authorised database administrators. Approved contact fields and approved review display names, ratings, comments and service months are visible to authenticated, invited members. Members must keep them within the group, but access control cannot prevent screenshots or copying by someone with access. Moderation is manual; there are no automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

Storage and retention

The database is private and administered through the operator’s Google account. Pending and rejected submissions are reviewed for deletion within 90 days; records necessary for an unresolved report or dispute may be retained until it is resolved and any applicable legal retention period ends. Approved contacts and reviews remain while relevant to the directory and are reviewed when a correction, objection or removal request is received. Contact us to request an individual assessment. Service-provider logs and backups follow the provider’s own retention arrangements.

Google services and recipients

When the directory loads, your browser connects to Google Apps Script. Submitting a form sends its contents to Google for processing and storage in the private Sheet. Authorised administrators and hosting or communications providers may receive data for operating the service; approved records are available to invited members. Google may process technical information such as IP address, browser details, cookies and service logs. We do not receive visitors’ Google passwords or general access to their Google accounts.

Google and other providers may process data outside your country or the EEA. Their applicable agreements and privacy notices describe processing locations and transfer safeguards. See Google’s privacy policy and Google’s data-processing terms where applicable. We do not claim that this website’s code controls all provider logs, cookies or backups.

Private membership and sign-in

The private Members sheet contains invited phone numbers, an optional name and an Active or Blocked status. It is visible to database administrators, not other members. The administrator generates a one-use 8-digit code and sends it personally to the intended number on WhatsApp. No WhatsApp API, automatic message sending, group-member import or access to your WhatsApp account is used.

Codes expire after 30 minutes or five unsuccessful attempts. Only a keyed hash is held on the server; the plaintext code appears in the administrator’s temporary dialog and the WhatsApp message. Session tokens are stored as hashes with the member phone number and expiration on the server for up to 7 days. Expired server records are removed during later code generation; provider backups may persist under provider policies. Blocking a number takes effect on subsequent server requests. Previously viewed or copied information cannot be recalled. We use membership and session data to manage requested access and protect the group directory under our legitimate interests, subject to your rights. Membership records are reviewed when access ends and removed when no longer needed for access management or a specific unresolved report.

Directory search

Search and service filters run in your browser. The current website does not send search text to an AI provider or store it. AI assistance is disabled.

Cookies and external services

Our own directory code stores a sign-in session token in this browser’s local storage for up to 7 days, where browser settings permit. This is used only to maintain requested access, is removed by Sign out, and expires on the server. Storage can be unavailable or temporary in embedded or private browsing contexts. The code does not install analytics, load advertising trackers or embed YouTube videos. Google supplies the embedded directory and may use cookies or related technologies to provide and secure its service. See Google’s cookie information. WhatsApp and other external contacts are ordinary links: the external service is opened when you click. Its own privacy rules then apply. See WhatsApp's privacy policy. This statement concerns our code, not a promise that hosting or external providers never process data.

Optional analytics, advertising trackers and video embeds are not enabled. If introduced, they will be reviewed separately and will require prior consent where applicable.

Messages and reports

If you email or WhatsApp us, we receive the details you choose to provide, including contact information and message content. We use enquiries to respond to your request or take steps toward a requested service, where Article 6(1)(b) GDPR applies. We handle general reports, security and correspondence on our legitimate interests in administering and protecting the service, subject to your rights, and comply with legal duties where Article 6(1)(c) applies. We do not use these enquiries for unrelated marketing without a separate lawful basis.

Correspondence is reviewed when a matter closes and deleted when no longer necessary. Where a legal duty or a specific dispute requires retention, the relevant records are kept only for that purpose and the applicable period. Provider-controlled retention is described in provider notices.

Your rights

Depending on the processing and legal basis, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, object to legitimate-interest processing, and withdraw any consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. We may need proportionate information to verify a request. You may complain to your supervisory authority, including Poland's UODO or Italy's Garante. These rights are subject to the conditions and exceptions in the GDPR.
