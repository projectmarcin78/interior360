Report a problem

Effective: 21 October 2026

Send a report to marcin@interior360.pl. The link opens your email application; nothing is submitted until you send the message.

What to include

  • The exact page URL and the profile name. For a review, include its author display name, work month and the relevant text so we can locate it.
  • Whether you are reporting unlawful content, a breach of our rules, incorrect business details, a privacy issue or a moderation decision.
  • A specific explanation and relevant supporting information. If you consider the content unlawful, explain why and identify the law if you know it.
  • Your name and a reply email, where applicable. Do not send unnecessary private documents or information about other people.
  • For an unlawful-content notice, confirm that you believe in good faith that the information and allegations are accurate and complete.

How reports are handled

We review reports and may request clarification. Where contact details are available, we acknowledge receipt and communicate the decision and reasons. You may request reconsideration by replying to our response. We do not remove an opinion simply because it is critical, and reporting content does not guarantee removal. Restrictions can be appropriate for unlawful content or a breach of the published rules.

Privacy and business corrections

You can use the same email to request correction, object to a listing or exercise data-protection rights. Explain your relationship to the profile. If ownership verification is necessary, we will request proportionate evidence privately.

Do not post your report as a public review. See Privacy & cookies for how correspondence is handled.
