Profiles and public information

Submit accurate business details that you are entitled to share. A business phone number or public listing can still contain personal data. Public availability alone does not authorise every reuse. We assess proposed listings before publication. Contact us to correct information, object to processing or request a review of a profile.

Writing a review

Describe your own experience and distinguish facts from your personal assessment. Do not submit paid, fabricated or coordinated reviews, undisclosed competitor reviews, duplicate content, advertising, threats, discriminatory remarks or abusive language. Do not disclose private addresses, identification documents or other people's private information. Describe specific events rather than making unsupported allegations of criminal conduct.

Moderation and verification

Submissions require manual approval. Positive and negative opinions are assessed by the same published rules. A negative rating or disagreement with a review does not by itself justify removal. We do not offer paid removal or preferential moderation for advertisers. We may refuse or restrict content that breaches these rules or applicable law.

We do not verify reviewer identity or confirm that a service took place. Approval is a content-moderation decision, not proof that a review is true. A reviewer must not describe their review as verified by this service.

Ratings and presentation

A profile score is the arithmetic mean of its published, approved ratings from 1 to 5, displayed to one decimal place. Pending and rejected reviews are excluded. Profiles without reviews have no score. Results are filtered by the selected town and trade and the search text; their order is not an independent quality ranking. Interior360's own services are promoted in a separate labelled section. Paid placements, if introduced, must be labelled Sponsored and do not alter review scores.

Problems and decisions

Use Report a problem for unlawful content, privacy concerns, corrections or moderation questions. Explain the issue and identify the content. We assess reports individually; reporting does not automatically remove content. Ask for reconsideration by email if you disagree with a decision. These rules do not restrict access to courts, regulators or other remedies available under law.
